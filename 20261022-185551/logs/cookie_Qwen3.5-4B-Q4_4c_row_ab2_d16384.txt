What is the best cookie in the world?  1. **Subjectivity of Taste** – “Best” is inherently subjective; different people have different preferences for texture, sweetness, flavor, and ingredients.  2. **Criteria for a “Best” Cookie** – If we try to define a set of criteria that many people consider desirable, they include:  - **Texture**: Crisp edges with a soft, chewy center.  - **Flavor**: Balanced sweetness with hints of vanilla, cinnamon, or other spices.  - **Ingredients**: High‑quality butter, flour, and a touch of sugar.  - **Versatility**: Works well in desserts, snacks
