What is the best cookie in the world?  That is a question that can only be answered by the person who is eating the cookie!  However, if we are looking for a definitive answer, we can look at the world of cookie science.  Cookie science is a fascinating field that explores the chemistry and physics behind the perfect cookie. It involves understanding how ingredients interact—the fat, the sugar, the flour, the leavening agents—to create the perfect texture, flavor, and spread.  So, what is the "best" cookie?  In cookie science, the "best" cookie is subjective. It depends entirely on the criteria you are using.  *
