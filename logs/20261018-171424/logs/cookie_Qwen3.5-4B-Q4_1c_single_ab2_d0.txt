What is the best cookie in the world?  1. **Subjectivity of Taste**   - Taste is a personal experience influenced by cultural background, individual preferences, and the specific ingredients used.   - Because “best” implies a universal standard, and no single palate can be declared superior to another, there is no objective metric for determining the world’s best cookie.  2. **Cultural Diversity**   - Different countries have iconic cookies that are celebrated locally and internationally (e.g., Italian *biscotti*, American chocolate‑chip, French *macarons*, Japanese *matcha* cookies).   - Each of these varieties has its own unique flavor profile, texture
